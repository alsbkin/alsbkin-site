How it works

1. Select your audio file.

2. Drag the blue selection handles or enter START/END times manually.

3. Preview the selection.

4. Optionally enable Fade In/Fade Out.

5. Choose WAV or MP3 and export for free.

Note: supported input formats depend on your browser and device. WAV export is generated natively. MP3 export is encoded locally in your browser using lame.js.
