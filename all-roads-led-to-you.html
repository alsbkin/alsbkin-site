About the song

“All Roads Led to You” is a song about the moment you realize that every road, every choice and every step has somehow brought you exactly where you were meant to be — beside the person you love. A story about two people choosing to keep moving forward together, hand in hand, not knowing where the road will lead, but knowing they want to stay by each other’s side.
