Extract Audio from Video

Create an audio file from a browser-supported video when you only need the soundtrack, speech, music or other audio contained in the clip. Select the video, choose an available audio output such as MP3 or WAV and export the extracted sound directly in your browser.

The quality of the result depends on the audio already contained in the source video and the output settings you choose. Converting to a higher bitrate cannot restore detail that was not present in the original soundtrack. WAV can be useful when you want an uncompressed output for further editing, while MP3 is more compact for everyday listening and sharing. Browser and device codec support determines which video files can be decoded.

Processing takes place locally in your browser rather than requiring the video to be uploaded to a remote processing server. If you only need a particular section, trim the source with the Video Cutter first or cut the extracted audio afterward with the Audio Cutter. For simple level changes, use the Audio Volume Booster.
