Reduce a video file when you need a smaller copy for sharing, storage or an upload limit. Select a browser-supported video, choose the available compression settings and export the processed version without installing a desktop editor.

The final size depends on factors such as duration, resolution, frame rate, source codec and the compression settings used. Smaller files generally require a trade-off in visual or audio quality, so preview the result when quality matters. A short clip may already be efficiently compressed and show only a modest reduction, while a high-resolution or high-bitrate source can offer more room to shrink. Browser and device capabilities also affect which formats can be decoded and encoded.

Processing is performed locally in your browser rather than by uploading the source video to a remote processing server. For the best balance, avoid repeatedly recompressing the same file. If you only need a shorter section, use the Video Cutter first; removing unnecessary footage can reduce size without compressing the remaining frames more aggressively.
