Change Video Audio Volume

Increase or reduce the audio level of a browser-supported video directly on your device. Load the clip, choose the volume adjustment, preview the result and export a new copy. This is useful when the picture is fine but the soundtrack is consistently too quiet or too loud.

A volume boost raises everything in the soundtrack, including background noise. If the original audio is already close to its maximum level, an excessive increase can introduce clipping or distortion, so moderate adjustments are safer. Reducing volume can help when a clip is much louder than other material in the same project. Browser and device capabilities determine which formats can be decoded and how efficiently the output is processed.

The video is processed locally in your browser instead of being uploaded to a remote processing server. This tool changes overall level; it does not remove noise, remix dialogue and music, or perform mastering. If the clip needs trimming, use the Video Cutter. If you only need its soundtrack, use Video to MP3 / WAV.
