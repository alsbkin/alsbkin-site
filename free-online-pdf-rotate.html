Rotate PDF Pages Online

Correct sideways or upside-down PDF pages directly in your browser. Load a PDF, choose the rotation direction and apply it to the pages supported by the tool before downloading the corrected document. Rotation is useful for scans, photographed documents and files assembled from pages with inconsistent orientation.

A 90-degree rotation is appropriate when a page is turned sideways, while 180 degrees corrects a page that is upside down. Check the preview or resulting PDF before replacing an important original, particularly when only selected pages needed adjustment. Rotation changes page orientation; it does not perform OCR, rewrite text or crop the document. Large PDFs may take longer because the work is performed by your browser and device.

The PDF is processed locally instead of being uploaded to a remote processing server. If the problem is page order rather than orientation, use the PDF Page Organizer. To keep only certain pages, use the PDF Split & Page Extractor, and to combine separate documents use the PDF Merger.
