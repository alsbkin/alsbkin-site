How it works

1. Select an audio file.

2. The tool analyzes tempo and harmonic content locally in your browser.

3. Press Play or SPACE to listen to the song.

4. Drag the white playhead or click anywhere on the waveform to move through the song.

5. Watch the current-key estimate update during playback and review the detected key changes in chronological order.

BPM is estimated with true spectral flux, onset peaks, a tempogram, beat-grid validation and half-time / double-time checks. Low confidence means the rhythm evidence is conflicting.

Note: BPM and musical-key detection are estimates. Complex arrangements, tempo changes, modulation, sparse percussion or ambiguous harmony can reduce accuracy.
