Private meeting transcription in your browser

This tool is designed for meetings, interviews, notes and spoken discussions where you want a written transcript without manually typing every sentence. It captures microphone audio only after you grant permission, converts speech into text in short chunks and places the result directly in the transcript editor.

Local processing: the speech-to-text model runs inside your browser using WebGPU when available and WebAssembly as a fallback. Audio is kept in memory long enough to process each chunk and is then discarded. This page does not send meeting audio or transcript text to an ALSBKIN server. Your browser may cache the speech model itself so it does not need to download it every time.

Languages and export: you can use automatic language detection or explicitly select Romanian, English or French. Timestamps and paragraph breaks can be included, speaker markers can be inserted manually, and the final text can be edited before export. Word and TXT files are generated locally in the browser and downloaded directly to your device.
