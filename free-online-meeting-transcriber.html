Meeting transcription in your browser

This tool is designed for meetings, interviews, notes and spoken discussions where you want a written transcript without manually typing every sentence. It captures microphone audio only after you grant permission, converts speech into text in short chunks and places the result directly in the transcript editor.

Speech recognition: this tool uses the speech-recognition service provided by your browser. Depending on your browser, operating system and installed language support, audio may be processed on-device or sent to the browser provider’s online speech service. ALSBKIN does not store meeting audio or transcript text on its own servers.

Languages, export and recovery: Romanian is selected by default, while English or French can be selected explicitly. The transcript is automatically saved in browser storage for recovery after an accidental close. You can also select one local TXT backup file that is overwritten with the current transcript every minute. Word and TXT exports are generated locally in the browser.
