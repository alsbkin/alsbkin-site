About the song

“Uncared For” is inspired by Augustus Edwin Mulready’s 1871 painting of the same name. The song looks beyond the poverty depicted in the scene and focuses on the dignity, exhaustion and humanity of children trying to earn their living while the world passes them by. Behind them, a wall is covered with grand words and distant concerns, while the reality unfolding right in front of it remains almost invisible. More than 150 years later, the image remains a strikingly relevant metaphor for how human attention can be drawn to what is grand, distant or loud while quiet realities nearby remain unseen.
