Crop a Video Online

Crop a browser-supported video when you need to remove unwanted edges or focus the frame on a smaller area. Load the video, choose the crop region and export the adjusted copy directly from your browser. Cropping can help remove empty borders, reframe a shot or prepare footage for a different layout.

Cropping changes the visible frame, not the duration of the video. Choose the crop carefully so important subjects, captions and interface elements remain inside the final image. A very small crop region may produce a lower-resolution result because fewer source pixels remain. Browser and device capabilities determine which source formats can be decoded and how efficiently the new video can be processed.

The source video is handled locally in your browser instead of being uploaded to a remote processing server. If the framing is correct but the clip is too long, use the Video Cutter. If the picture is sideways, use the Video Rotator & Flipper. For a smaller file without changing the framing, use the Video Compressor.
