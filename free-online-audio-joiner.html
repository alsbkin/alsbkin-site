Join and Merge Audio Files

Combine multiple supported audio files into one continuous output file directly in your browser. This is useful for joining song sections, voice recordings, podcast segments, sound effects or other clips that need to play in a specific order. Add the files, arrange them in the sequence you want and export the combined result.

Before merging, listen to the source clips and check that their beginnings and endings are clean. Large differences in volume between clips will remain noticeable after joining, so it can help to adjust levels beforehand when necessary. The joiner focuses on combining files; it does not automatically master, normalize or remix the material. Browser and device codec support can affect which source formats can be decoded.

Processing is handled locally in the browser rather than by uploading the audio to a remote processing server. If a clip needs trimming before it is joined, use the Audio Cutter first. For smoother transitions at the boundaries, the Audio Fade tool can add fade-ins or fade-outs.
