Cookie Policy

This page explains how ALSBKIN.ro may use cookies, browser storage and similar technologies, what third-party services can set their own identifiers, and how visitors can control those technologies.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a browser when a website or a third-party service needs to remember information between requests or visits. Similar functions can be provided by local storage, session storage or device identifiers. Not every ALSBKIN page needs cookies to display its main content.

2. Essential and preference storage

ALSBKIN pages may store simple preferences such as the selected interface language. These values help keep the same language or interface choice when you move between pages. Removing site data in your browser resets those preferences.

3. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

Some non-tool pages may load Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use advertising cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on a visitor's prior visits to ALSBKIN.ro or other websites, subject to the visitor's choices and applicable law. The dedicated free-online tool pages do not load the AdSense script.

You can manage or opt out of personalised Google advertising through Google Ads Settings. For additional information, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where Google requires consent in the EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland, consent should be collected through a Google-certified consent management platform. Any consent interface displayed for Google advertising operates in addition to this policy.

4. YouTube and other embedded services

Videos or music players can come from external platforms. ALSBKIN often uses privacy-enhanced YouTube embedding where practical, but activating an embed can still create a direct connection with the provider. Spotify and social-media links or embeds may also use their own cookies or account identifiers according to their independent policies.

5. Analytics and measurement

If measurement or analytics services are added or changed, they may use cookies or comparable technologies to understand aggregate traffic and performance. This policy should be read together with any consent interface shown on the site and the Privacy Policy.

6. How to control cookies

You can delete or block cookies from your browser settings and clear site data stored for ALSBKIN.ro. Most browsers also allow you to restrict third-party cookies. Blocking storage can reset language preferences or prevent an embedded service from functioning as expected, but the basic music pages should remain readable.

7. Changes to this policy

The technologies used by the website can change as new tools, embeds or advertising features are introduced. This page will be updated when a material change affects how cookies or comparable technologies are used.
